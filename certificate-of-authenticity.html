COLLECTING WITH KIN

Certificates of Authenticity

Every original artwork and Fine Art Print acquired through KIN Contemporary is accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity (COA), providing assurance of the artwork's authenticity, provenance and production details.

A Certificate of Authenticity is an important record that confirms the work has been acquired through KIN Contemporary and has been authorised by the artist.

We recommend retaining your certificate in a safe place, as it forms an important part of the artwork's provenance and may be required for insurance, valuation or future resale.

01

Original Artworks

Every original artwork is supplied with a Certificate of Authenticity issued by KIN Contemporary and authorised by the artist.

Each certificate includes:

  • Artist's name
  • Artwork title
  • Year of creation
  • Medium
  • Dimensions
  • Confirmation that the work is an original artwork
  • Artist's signature (where applicable)
  • Certificate number
  • Date of issue

The Certificate of Authenticity forms part of the artwork's permanent provenance and should always accompany the work if ownership changes.

02

Fine Art Prints

Every artist-authorised Fine Art Print sold through KIN Contemporary is accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity confirming its authenticity and production details.

Each certificate includes:

  • Artist's name
  • Artwork title
  • Year
  • Print dimensions
  • Archival printing process
  • Paper or substrate
  • Edition type (Limited Edition or Open Edition)
  • Edition number (for Limited Editions)
  • Artist's signature (where applicable)
  • Certificate number
  • Date of issue

Limited Editions

Limited Edition Fine Art Prints are produced in strictly limited quantities determined in collaboration with the artist.

Each print is individually numbered and accompanied by a corresponding Certificate of Authenticity. Once an edition has sold out, no further prints from that edition will be produced.

Open Editions

Open Edition Fine Art Prints are produced exclusively by KIN Contemporary with the artist's authorisation using museum-quality archival materials and professional printing standards.

Each print is accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity confirming its provenance and authenticity.

03

Museum-Quality Production

KIN Contemporary partners with leading professional Fine Art printers to produce museum-quality reproductions using archival pigment inks and premium conservation-grade papers and substrates.

Every print is produced to exceptional archival standards, ensuring outstanding colour accuracy, longevity and faithful reproduction of the artist's original work.

04

Provenance

Provenance plays an important role in the long-term value and history of an artwork.

Your Certificate of Authenticity provides an important record of ownership and should be retained with any purchase documentation.

If the artwork is sold or gifted in the future, we recommend that the Certificate of Authenticity accompanies the work to preserve its provenance.

A NOTE FROM KIN CONTEMPORARY

Authenticity extends beyond a certificate.

At KIN Contemporary, authenticity reflects our commitment to representing artists with integrity, producing Fine Art Prints to the highest archival standards, and ensuring every work is presented with the care and respect it deserves.
